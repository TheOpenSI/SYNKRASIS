Clinical Impression
Left homonymous hemianopia (stroke aetiology) causing a neuro-visual field loss phenotype. Preserved central acuity does not reflect the mobility impact of the hemianopic field loss, which explains left-sided collisions and the recent fall.
Management Plan
Continue OT-led compensatory scanning and environmental cueing.
Communicate findings to rehabilitation team and GP.
Recommended use of a support guide or extra caution with turning, doorway navigation, and crowded indoor spaces.
Consider use of mobility cane and/or low vision identification badge. 
Driving remains inappropriate without formal medical and visual field clearance.
Review in 6 months or sooner if functional status changes or new visual symptoms
