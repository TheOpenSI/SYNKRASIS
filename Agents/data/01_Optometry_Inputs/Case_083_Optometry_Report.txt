Clinical Impression
Progression of normal-tension glaucoma OU, Moderate RE, Advanced LE Severity staging (Optometry Australia). causing inferior field / spatial awareness loss phenotype. Automated visual field testing showed arcuate loss OD with paracentral inferior loss OS, demonstrating structure-function correlation and consistent with the reported side-collision risk.
Management Plan
