==============================
Case 064
==============================
1. Patient Context
    • Age: 82
    • Setting: Rehabilitation outpatient clinic
    • Referral context: Fall after missing a doorway on the left
    • Key diagnosis: Stroke-related left homonymous hemianopia
2. Key Vision Issues
    • Left visual field loss may cause missed doorframes, obstacles, and people on the left.
    • Preserved central clarity may underestimate mobility impact.
    • Reading may be affected by difficulty locating the start of lines.
3. Functional Impact
    • Mobility/Falls: Turning into doorways and navigating crowds may be unsafe without consistent left scanning.
    • ADLs/IADLs: Reading instructions, menus, or forms may be slower due to left-line initiation difficulty.
    • Participation: Busy environments may be tiring and reduce confidence.
    • Environment: Left-sided hazards near doorways and corridors are priority concerns.
4. Vision-Informed OT Considerations
Mobility Strategies
    • Train: pause before turning → scan to left wall/doorframe → locate floor opening → then enter, supporting safer doorway navigation.
Environmental Modifications
    • Place a bright vertical cue strip on the left edge of frequently used doorframes to prompt leftward search during room transitions.
Task Adaptations
    • Use a left-margin marker on reading materials to help locate line starts during home and therapy tasks.
5. Falls Risk Level
    • High vision-related falls risk — field loss has already contributed to a fall during doorway navigation.
6. Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings
    • Assess scanning consistency during transfers, turns, reading, and crowded-space mobility.
    • Distinguish visual field loss from neglect or broader perceptual issues during OT assessment.
