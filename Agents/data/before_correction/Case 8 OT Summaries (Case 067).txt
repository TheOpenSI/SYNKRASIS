==============================
Case 067
==============================
1. Patient Context
    • Age: 79
    • Setting: Community optometry following GP referral
    • Referral context: Fall on poorly lit outdoor step with severe blur and glare
    • Key diagnosis: Dense bilateral cataract causing markedly reduced clarity, contrast, and glare tolerance
2. Key Vision Issues
    • Severe blur may limit detection of step edges and obstacles.
    • Glare and poor low-light vision may increase risk at dusk or near bright-dark transitions.
    • Bifocal use on steps may further reduce safe ground-level judgement.
3. Functional Impact
    • Mobility/Falls: Outdoor steps, patio edges, and dusk mobility may be unsafe without supports.
    • ADLs/IADLs: Reading small print and transport access may require assistance.
    • Participation/Confidence: Reluctance to go outside at dusk may reflect vision-related risk awareness.
    • Environment: Poorly lit entry and patio areas are high-priority hazards.
4. Vision-Informed OT Considerations
Lighting and Contrast
    • Install bright sensor lighting over the patio step and apply high-contrast tape to the step edge to support edge detection.
Mobility Strategies
    • Practise: pause before outdoor step → locate contrast strip → place foot fully on next level → then transfer weight.
Task Adaptations
    • For small print, consider large-print household labels and consistent document placement until cataract care progresses.
5. Falls Risk Level
    • High vision-related falls risk — severe bilateral blur and glare have already contributed to a step-related fall.
6. Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings
    • Prioritise step safety, dusk routines, and transport dependence.
    • OT assessment should consider lighting, mobility aids, confidence, and support availability.
