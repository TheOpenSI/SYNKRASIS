==============================
Case 083
==============================
1. Patient Context
    • Age: 78
    • Setting: Shared-care glaucoma review
    • Referral context: Increasing side collisions without recent fall
    • Key diagnosis: Progressive normal-tension glaucoma causing peripheral/spatial awareness loss
2. Key Vision Issues
    • Peripheral field loss may reduce awareness of objects approaching from the side.
    • Inferior field loss may affect detection of floor-level clutter and baskets.
    • Reduced contrast may worsen navigation in dim hallways.
3. Functional Impact
    • Mobility/Falls: May clip doorframes, laundry baskets, or hallway objects, especially when turning.
    • ADLs/IADLs: Carrying laundry or moving through cluttered areas may require more visual checking.
    • Participation/Confidence: Caution in cluttered spaces may reflect reduced side awareness.
    • Environment: Hallways, doorframes, and laundry areas are priority risk locations.
4. Vision-Informed OT Considerations
Environmental Modifications
    • Remove laundry baskets from hallway edges and keep door approaches clear to reduce side-collision risk.
Lighting and Contrast
    • Add plug-in night lights at hallway bends and bathroom entry to support detection of low-level obstacles.
Mobility Strategies
    • Train: pause before turning → scan toward both doorframe edges → check floor space → turn with a wider arc.
5. Falls Risk Level
    • Moderate vision-related falls risk — progressive peripheral field loss is linked with side collisions but no recent fall.
6. Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings
    • Assess turning, doorway navigation, and clutter management.
    • Integrate vision findings with mobility, confidence, driving restrictions, and home supports.
