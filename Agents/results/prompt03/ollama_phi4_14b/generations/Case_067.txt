==============================
Case 067
==============================

1. **Patient Context**

- **Age:** 79
- **Gender:** Male
- **Care Setting:** Community optometry practice following GP referral
- **Falls Referral Context:** Referred after a fall on a poorly lit step due to severe blur and glare.
- **Key Vision Diagnosis:** Dense bilateral cataract leading to blur and reduced visual clarity, significantly impacting vision and contributing to falls.
- **Other Contextual Factors:** Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and hypertension, using inhalers and antihypertensive therapy; relies on wife for transport and reading small print.

2. **Key Vision Issues**

- **Severe Blur and Glare:** Dense nuclear sclerosis with posterior subcapsular cataract causing significant visual impairment, including poor contrast sensitivity and step-edge visibility in low light.
- **Vision Acuity Loss:** Marked reduction in both distance and near visual acuity, which may affect tasks requiring clear vision.
- **Low Light Visibility:** Increased risk of falls due to decreased ability to see step edges in dim lighting.

3. **Functional Impact**

- **Mobility and Falls:** Difficulty detecting step edges, particularly in low light or glare conditions, likely contributing to falls. May affect safe navigation of stairs and uneven surfaces.
- **ADLs/IADLs:** Challenges with tasks requiring clear vision, such as reading small print, using bifocals, and identifying objects at a distance, potentially necessitating assistance.
- **Participation / Confidence / Roles:** Reliance on wife for transportation and reading may affect independence and confidence in performing community and household tasks.
- **Environmental Interaction:** Poor lighting conditions and glare may exacerbate visibility issues, affecting mobility and task performance.

4. **Vision-Informed OT Considerations**

- **Mobility Strategies:**
  - Assess mobility in different lighting conditions and recommend strategies for safe navigation.
  - Consider Orientation & Mobility (O&M) assessment to address specific mobility challenges.

- **Environmental Modifications / Home Environment:**
  - Evaluate home lighting, particularly around entryways and staircases, to enhance visibility.
  - Suggest high-contrast edge markings on steps to improve edge detection in low light.

- **Lighting and Contrast:**
  - Assess lighting needs and recommend modifications to reduce glare and improve illumination where necessary.

- **Task Adaptations:**
  - Explore adaptations for tasks like reading and using digital devices, such as larger print materials or screen magnification.

- **Education and Support:**
  - Educate the patient and wife on home safety modifications and strategies to minimize fall risk.
  - Discuss the impact of vision on daily activities and explore support options.

- **Clinical Follow-up / Communication:**
  - Maintain communication with optometry to monitor changes in vision and adjust recommendations as needed.
  - Coordinate with ophthalmology regarding post-surgical vision improvements and their impact on functional tasks.

**Falls Risk Level: High**
- Due to severe visual impairment and recent fall history, vision-related falls risk is high. The patient experiences significant blur and glare, impacting step-edge visibility in low light, increasing fall risk.

5. **Summary of Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings**

- Assess mobility strategies and environmental modifications to enhance safety in low-light conditions.
- Explore task adaptations to support reading and close work activities.
- Provide education on home safety and fall prevention strategies to the patient and support persons.
