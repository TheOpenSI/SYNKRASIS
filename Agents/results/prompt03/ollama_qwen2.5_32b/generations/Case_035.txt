==============================
Case 035
==============================

1. Patient Context

- Age: 67
- Care setting: shared-care glaucoma review in community optometry
- Falls pathway / referral context: routine review where worsening obstacle awareness was identified; no confirmed falls, but several near-misses reported in dim hallways.
- Key vision diagnosis: Moderate glaucoma OU causing peripheral field loss and spatial awareness deficits.
- Other context: Hypertension and obstructive sleep apnoea; drives daytime and occasional dusk trips.

2. Key Vision Issues

- Peripheral visual field loss with specific inferior nasal arcuate defects in both eyes, affecting obstacle awareness and spatial orientation.
- Reduced contrast sensitivity, contributing to difficulties in dim lighting conditions.
- Spatial awareness complaints consistent with glaucoma progression, impacting confidence in low-level hazard detection.

3. Functional Impact

**Mobility and Falls**
- Diminished obstacle detection, particularly on uneven surfaces and in low light conditions.
- Potential difficulties stepping around low furniture and in dark hallways, leading to near-misses.
- Risk of falls or near-falls may be increased during nighttime or in unfamiliar dimly lit environments.

**ADLs/IADLs**
- Challenged in locating and navigating around floor-level objects, which may affect tasks such as cleaning or retrieving items from the floor.
- Reduced confidence in low light settings may impact tasks such as preparing medication in the evening.

**Participation / Confidence / Roles**
- Reduced confidence in navigating through environments with low-level hazards, potentially affecting community mobility and engagement in activities.
- Diminished independence in managing household tasks that require low-level vision.

**Environmental Interaction**
- Low-level lighting in hallways and living areas may need to be improved for enhanced safety.
- Potential for visual clutter in task environments to exacerbate spatial awareness difficulties.

4. Vision-Informed OT Considerations

- **Mobility Strategies**
  - Assess the person's confidence and ability to navigate through environments with low-level hazards, particularly in low light.
  - Consider O&M assessment or training for route learning, obstacle awareness, and safe community travel strategies.

- **Environmental Modifications / Home Environment**
  - Evaluate lighting levels and glare in areas where low-level hazards are present, such as hallways and living areas.
  - Recommend removal or clear delineation of floor clutter and low-level obstacles to reduce tripping hazards.

- **Task Adaptations**
  - Assess how the person locates and interacts with floor-level objects, and provide strategies or adaptive equipment to improve safety and confidence.
  - Consider the impact of low-level lighting on tasks such as preparing medication or engaging in household management.

- **Education and Support**
  - Educate the person and informal supports about the importance of consistent low-level lighting and obstacle-free pathways.
  - Provide guidance on maintaining a predictable environment to enhance spatial awareness and confidence.

- **Clinical Follow-up / Communication**
  - Liaise with O&M for comprehensive mobility assessment and training.
  - Collaborate with ophthalmology for updates on glaucoma management and progression.

5. Falls Risk Level

- **Moderate**: Given the peripheral field loss and spatial awareness deficits, particularly in low light conditions, the risk of falls is moderate. However, the patient has no confirmed falls, only near-misses, and remains active.

6. Summary of Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings

- Mobility and obstacle awareness assessment in low light conditions.
- Environmental modifications to improve lighting and reduce clutter.
- O&M assessment or training to enhance safe mobility.
- Education and support for managing spatial awareness challenges.
