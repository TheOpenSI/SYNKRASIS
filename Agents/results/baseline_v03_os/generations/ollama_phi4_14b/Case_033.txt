==============================
Case 033
==============================

1. **Patient Context**

The patient is a 63-year-old female attending a community optometry practice for a routine eye examination. She reports hesitancy when walking on uneven surfaces without her glasses, though no falls have been confirmed. Her main ocular concern is an under-correction of myopia, leading to blurred vision when she forgets to wear her glasses while walking.

2. **Key Vision Issues**

- **Under-corrected myopic refractive error:** The patient experiences reduced visual clarity without her glasses, particularly when walking or navigating uneven surfaces.
- **Inconsistent spectacle use:** The patient habitually removes her glasses for near tasks and continues walking without them, which contributes to visual impairment during mobility.
- **Early nuclear sclerosis:** While the patient’s lens changes are not severe, they are a noted finding but not currently affecting visual function significantly.

3. **Functional Impact**

- **Mobility and Falls:** 
  - **Obstacle detection and surface changes:** Difficulty in judging garden path edges and hesitancy on uneven paving suggest challenges in detecting obstacles and adjusting to surface changes.
  - **Low-light conditions:** The patient reports needing to slow down outdoors, especially in dull weather, indicating potential issues with mobility in low-light environments.
  
- **Participation / Confidence / Roles:**
  - **Community access and independence:** The patient’s reluctance to drive or walk without glasses may affect her confidence in community activities and overall independence.

- **Environmental Interaction:**
  - **Lighting and contrast:** The need to slow down in dull weather suggests that lighting and contrast could impact her visual performance during mobility tasks.

4. **Vision-Informed OT Considerations**

- **Self-care:**
  - Assess whether the patient’s inconsistent spectacle use affects medication management or other self-care tasks requiring visual acuity.

- **Environmental Modifications / Home Environment:**
  - Assess household lighting, including brightness, glare, and shadowing, to ensure optimal visual conditions for activities both indoors and outdoors.

- **Mobility Strategies:**
  - Evaluate mobility routines and the patient’s adherence to wearing spectacles consistently during all tasks, especially walking. 
  - Consider the potential benefits of Orientation & Mobility (O&M) training to improve safety strategies when navigating uneven surfaces or low-light environments.

- **Task Adaptations:**
  - Assess whether removing glasses for near tasks inadvertently affects other activities, such as reading labels or using digital devices, and explore task adaptations to minimize the need for removal.

- **Education and Support:**
  - Educate the patient on the importance of consistent spectacle use, particularly when moving from near to distance tasks, to enhance safety and confidence.
  - Discuss the potential benefits and adaptation process of multifocal lenses as recommended, ensuring understanding of how they can support her visual needs.

- **Driving / Licensing / Transport:**
  - Address the patient’s adaptation to using distance vision correction as required for her driving license, and explore alternative transportation options if necessary during the adaptation period.

- **Falls Risk Level:**
  - Moderate: The patient’s visual impairment due to under-correction and inconsistent spectacle use contributes to hesitancy on uneven surfaces, indicating a moderate risk of falls.

5. **Summary of Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings**

- Assess the impact of inconsistent spectacle use on ADLs, particularly medication management and mobility safety.
- Evaluate the need for environmental modifications, focusing on lighting and contrast, to support visual performance.
- Educate on the importance of consistent spectacle use and explore potential adaptations for multifocal lenses to enhance visual clarity during both near and distance tasks.
- Consider O&M training to improve safe navigation strategies in various environments.
