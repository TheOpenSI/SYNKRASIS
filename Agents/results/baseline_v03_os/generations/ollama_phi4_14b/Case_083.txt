==============================
Case 083
==============================

1. **Patient Context**

- **Age:** 78
- **Gender:** Female
- **Care Setting:** Shared-care glaucoma review in community optometry
- **Diagnosis:** Normal-tension glaucoma OU, with progressive visual field loss impacting spatial awareness.
- **Modifiers:** Family history of glaucoma; cardiovascular disease.
- **Falls Pathway/Referral Context:** Routine follow-up identified increasing side-collision history. Patient reports clipping doorframes and laundry baskets but no recent falls.

2. **Key Vision Issues**

- Peripheral field loss OU, particularly inferior arcuate and paracentral defects, affecting spatial awareness.
- Reduced contrast sensitivity, possibly impacting object detection and differentiation.
- Visual field defects correlating with reported spatial awareness difficulties.
- Advanced glaucoma severity OU with structural damage evidenced by RNFL thinning and visual field loss.

3. **Functional Impact**

**Mobility and Falls**
- Inferior peripheral vision loss may affect obstacle detection, increasing risk of collision with objects such as doorframes and laundry baskets.
- Difficulty navigating tight spaces and cluttered environments due to reduced spatial awareness.

**ADLs/IADLs**
- Potentially affected tasks include:
  - **Cooking and Heat/Blade Safety:** Difficulty in locating and identifying objects or hazards in peripheral vision.
  - **Grooming and Personal Care:** Challenges in locating grooming tools or personal items.
  - **Medication Management:** Risk of missing or misidentifying medication containers.

**Participation / Confidence / Roles**
- Increased caution in environments, potentially reducing confidence in community participation.
- Avoidance of activities perceived as risky, possibly impacting social participation and roles.

**Environmental Interaction**
- **Lighting:** Poor lighting may exacerbate spatial awareness issues.
- **Clutter:** Increased risk in cluttered environments, necessitating decluttering.

4. **Vision-Informed OT Considerations**

**A. Self-care**
- Assess ability to safely locate and manage personal care items and medications.

**B. Environmental Modifications / Home Environment**
- Recommend decluttering, particularly in hallways and high-traffic areas.
- Advise deliberate scanning techniques before turning or navigating through spaces.

**C. Lighting and Contrast**
- Assess and improve hallway lighting to enhance spatial awareness.
- Consider contrasting colors for critical objects and pathways.

**D. Mobility Strategies**
- Consider referral for Orientation & Mobility (O&M) assessment to develop safe navigation strategies.
- Educate on scanning techniques to compensate for peripheral vision loss.

**E. Assistive Technology / Non-visual Strategies**
- Evaluate potential for assistive devices that enhance spatial awareness.
- Consider non-visual strategies for task management.

**F. Task Adaptation**
- Assess kitchen and grooming tasks for safety risks due to spatial awareness limitations.
- Recommend consistent placement of frequently used items to reduce reliance on peripheral vision.

**G. Support, Carers and Formal Services**
- Explore current informal support systems and their capacity to assist with navigation and safety.
- Educate family members on supporting safe navigation and consistent environmental setups.

**H. Participation and Community Access**
- Address potential social isolation due to reduced confidence in mobility.
- Explore alternative transportation options if driving restrictions are implemented.

**I. Driving / Licensing / Transport Rule**
- Acknowledge visual field loss may impact driving; suggest reassessment post specialist review and Esterman test.

**Falls Risk Level**
- **Moderate:** Due to peripheral vision loss affecting spatial awareness and increased side-collision incidents, though no recent falls reported.

5. **Summary of Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings**

- Prioritize assessment of spatial awareness impact on mobility and task safety.
- Implement environmental modifications such as improved lighting and decluttering.
- Educate on scanning techniques and consider O&M referral for safe navigation.
- Monitor and support participation in social roles and community activities.
