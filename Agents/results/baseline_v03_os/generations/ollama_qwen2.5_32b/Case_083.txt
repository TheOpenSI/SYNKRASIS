==============================
Case 083
==============================

1. Patient Context
- Age: 78, Female
- Care setting: shared-care glaucoma review in community optometry
- Falls pathway / referral context: routine glaucoma follow-up, increasing side-collision history; no recent falls but reports clipping doorframes and laundry baskets.
- Key vision diagnosis: Progression of normal-tension glaucoma OU, moderate RE, advanced LE, causing inferior field / spatial awareness loss.
- Other contextual impairment: cardiovascular disease, hypertension, migraine, Raynaud’s phenomenon.

2. Key Vision Issues
- Inferior and nasal field loss in the right eye (OD) and inferior field loss in the left eye (OS), impacting spatial awareness and side-collision risk.
- Reduced contrast sensitivity, affecting overall visual acuity and perception.
- Potential functional impact on driving, requiring further assessment.

3. Functional Impact

Mobility and Falls
- Obstacle detection in low-light environments or cluttered areas is impaired, increasing the risk of side-collision and near-misses.
- Hallway navigation and doorframe negotiation may be challenging, potentially leading to clipping and bumping into objects.
- Driving may be affected by reduced peripheral awareness and contrast sensitivity, necessitating further evaluation.

ADLs/IADLs
- Locating and organizing objects in cluttered environments may be difficult due to reduced peripheral vision and spatial awareness.
- Reading and near tasks may require more effort due to reduced contrast sensitivity and potential need for additional lighting.

Participation / Confidence / Roles
- Community access may be limited due to increased caution and reduced confidence in navigating unfamiliar or crowded environments.
- Leisure and social activities may be restricted due to mobility concerns and safety issues.

Environmental Interaction
- Hallway lighting and contrast enhancement may be necessary to improve obstacle detection and reduce the risk of side-collision.
- Storage consistency and visual clutter reduction in the home environment may support safe navigation.

4. Vision-Informed OT Considerations

- **Environmental Modifications / Home Environment**
  - Assess hallway lighting to ensure adequate brightness, reducing glare and shadowing that may impede obstacle detection.
  - Evaluate storage consistency and clutter in the home environment to support safe navigation and reduce the risk of side-collision.

- **Lighting and Contrast**
  - Assess current lighting levels and task lighting needs to improve contrast sensitivity and object detection, particularly for reading and near tasks.

- **Mobility Strategies**
  - Consider Orientation & Mobility (O&M) assessment or training to address navigation challenges in unfamiliar or busy environments, and to develop safe scanning strategies.
  - Recommend deliberate scanning techniques before turning in hallways to enhance spatial awareness and reduce collision risk.

- **Task Adaptation**
  - Assess how the patient locates and organizes objects in cluttered environments and recommend strategies to reduce clutter and enhance visibility.
  - Evaluate the patient’s current driving situation and discuss the need for further assessment using the Esterman test to determine fitness to drive.

- **Education and Support**
  - Educate the patient on the importance of consistent spectacle use and deliberate scanning to improve spatial awareness.
  - Provide guidance on decluttering and maintaining a predictable environment to support safe navigation.

5. Falls Risk Level
- **Moderate**
  - The progression of glaucoma with significant peripheral and spatial awareness loss increases the risk of side-collision and near-misses, particularly in cluttered or low-light environments.

6. Summary of Key OT Considerations from Vision Findings
- **Environmental modifications** to support safe navigation in the home.
- **Mobility strategies** including deliberate scanning and potential O&M assessment.
- **Lighting and contrast enhancement** to improve object detection and reading tasks.
- **Assessment of driving fitness** through the Esterman test.
