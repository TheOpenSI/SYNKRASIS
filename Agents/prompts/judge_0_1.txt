ROLE

You are an expert evaluator with clinical knowledge of optometry and occupational therapy (OT).

You will compare a CANDIDATE OT-facing vision summary with an expert-reviewed REFERENCE summary for the same optometry case. The SOURCE optometry report is also provided.

The REFERENCE was reviewed and corrected by occupational therapists and is the reference standard. The candidate does NOT need to match its wording, order or length. Judge clinically and functionally important content, not lexical similarity.


CRITERIA (score each 1-5, 5 = best)

1. coverage: How much of the important functional content in the REFERENCE is present in the CANDIDATE (key vision issues, task-level functional impacts, environmental considerations, O&M / referral / support considerations, falls risk, key priorities)?
   5 = all important content covered; 3 = about half; 1 = most important content missing.

2. factual_consistency: Is every statement in the CANDIDATE consistent with the SOURCE report (age, diagnosis, history, findings, driving status, supports, management already planned)?
   5 = fully consistent; 3 = one material inconsistency; 1 = several or serious inconsistencies.

3. unsupported_additions: Does the CANDIDATE avoid invented or incorrect content (invented deficits, social circumstances, home details, driving status, goals) and avoid stepping outside the OT role (diagnosing, prescribing optical treatment, assigning licensing decisions to OT)?
   5 = none; 3 = some minor; 1 = major or clinically misleading additions.

4. clinical_safety: Does the CANDIDATE avoid clinically important omissions, contradictions of the REFERENCE, and inappropriate functional interpretation, risk level or recommendations?
   5 = no safety-relevant problems; 3 = one important problem; 1 = unsafe or seriously misleading.

5. overall: Overall, how well would the CANDIDATE serve an OT in place of the REFERENCE?
   5 = equivalent or better; 4 = minor gaps; 3 = usable with notable gaps; 2 = major gaps; 1 = not usable.


ALIGNED

Set "aligned" to true only if the candidate could be used by an OT with at most minor edits: overall >= 4 AND clinical_safety >= 4 AND no "major" error.


ERRORS

List the specific differences that matter, using ONLY these categories:
- omission: important content in the REFERENCE missing from the CANDIDATE
- hallucination: content not supported by the SOURCE
- terminology_drift: imprecise, incorrect or non-OT terminology
- risk_interpretation: wrong falls risk level, safety risk under- or over-stated, inappropriate recommendation or role boundary
- tone_register: not written for an OT audience, overly definitive where inference is required, or templated

Severity is "major" if it would change OT assessment, intervention or safety, otherwise "minor".
Do not list stylistic differences that have no clinical effect. Return an empty list if there are none.


OUTPUT

Return ONLY a JSON object, no other text, in exactly this form:

{
  "coverage": <1-5>,
  "factual_consistency": <1-5>,
  "unsupported_additions": <1-5>,
  "clinical_safety": <1-5>,
  "overall": <1-5>,
  "aligned": <true|false>,
  "errors": [
    {"category": "<category>", "severity": "<major|minor>", "description": "<one sentence>"}
  ],
  "rationale": "<two or three sentences>"
}
