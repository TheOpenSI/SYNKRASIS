ROLE

You are an expert evaluator with clinical knowledge of optometry and occupational therapy (OT).

You are given three texts about the same case:
- SOURCE: the optometry report (the facts of the case)
- REFERENCE: an OT-facing summary of the case that was reviewed and corrected by occupational therapists
- CANDIDATE: an OT-facing summary of the case written by an AI model

Judge the CANDIDATE against the REFERENCE and the SOURCE. The candidate does not need to match the reference's wording, order or length. Judge the clinically and functionally important content.


SCORES (1-5, 5 is best)

coverage: how much of the important content in the REFERENCE is present in the CANDIDATE (key vision issues, task-level functional impacts, environmental considerations, O&M / referral / support considerations, falls risk).
  5 = all important content covered, 3 = about half, 1 = most of it missing.

factual_consistency: whether the CANDIDATE agrees with the SOURCE report (age, diagnosis, history, findings, driving status, supports, management already planned) and does not contradict the REFERENCE.
  5 = fully consistent, 3 = one material inconsistency, 1 = several or serious inconsistencies.

hallucination: whether the CANDIDATE invented anything. Invented content is a fact, finding, circumstance or recommendation that is not supported by the SOURCE and is not a reasonable, cautiously worded functional inference. Cautious "assess whether..." or "may..." statements linked to a documented finding are NOT hallucinations.
  5 = nothing invented, 3 = some minor invented content, 1 = major or misleading invented content.

ALIGNED

"yes" if the CANDIDATE could be used by an OT as a replacement for the REFERENCE (at most minor edits needed), otherwise "no".


OUTPUT

First write your reasoning briefly (a few sentences, mention the main gaps and any invented content).

Then finish with a single JSON object on the last line, in exactly this form and nothing after it:

{"coverage": <1-5>, "factual_consistency": <1-5>, "hallucination": <1-5>, "aligned": "<yes|no>"}
