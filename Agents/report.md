# Stage 2A.1 — Report

## 1. How judging works

Two LLM judges score every generated summary independently. Each judge receives:
- the **source** optometry report, to check facts
- the OT-corrected **reference** summary, to check content
- the **candidate**, which is the summary generated by the model being benchmarked

### Judging criteria

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, where 5 is best.

| Criterion | Question the judge answers | 5 means | 1 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | How much of the important OT content in the reference does the candidate include? This covers vision issues, task-level impacts, environment, O&M/referrals, support and falls risk. | everything important is covered | most is missing |
| Factual consistency | Does the candidate agree with the source report (age, diagnosis, history, driving, supports, planned management)? | fully consistent | several or serious inconsistencies |
| Unsupported additions | Does the candidate avoid invented content and stay within the OT role (no diagnosing, no prescribing)? | nothing invented | major or misleading inventions |
| Clinical safety | Does the candidate avoid important omissions, contradictions, and a wrong risk level or recommendation? | no safety problems | unsafe or seriously misleading |
| Overall | How well would the candidate serve an OT in place of the reference? | as good or better | not usable |

Each judge also gives a yes/no verdict, **aligned**: the candidate is usable by an OT with at most minor edits. That requires overall ≥ 4, clinical safety ≥ 4 and no major error.

Each judge also lists the specific errors it found. Each error is labelled with one of five categories:
- omission
- hallucination (unsupported content)
- terminology drift
- risk interpretation
- tone/register

Each error is also marked **major** if it would change OT assessment, intervention or safety, otherwise **minor**.

### Combining the two judges

| Result | Rule |
|---|---|
| Scores | average of the two judges |
| Final aligned | majority vote. With 2 judges, a 1–1 split counts as **False**, which is the conservative choice. |
| Needs review | **True** if the judges disagree on aligned, their overall scores differ by 2 or more, or a judge returned an unreadable answer. These cases should be checked by a person, because the LLM judge is not the only basis for decisions (protocol §4.3). |

### Checks that don't use a judge

- **Falls risk match:** the Low/Moderate/High level in the candidate's Section 5 must equal the reference's level.
- **ROUGE-L:** word-sequence overlap with the reference, from 0 to 1. It is supplementary only and does not measure clinical correctness.

## 3. Faults seen in v0.3 and what v0.4 may improve

The counts come from searching the 10 v0.3 outputs and the 10 OT references for each pattern. v0.4 is v0.3 with short generic additions and nothing removed. It has not been benchmarked yet.

| Fault in v0.3 output | v0.3 outputs | References | v0.4 change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt rule labels used as headings (e.g. "A. ASSESS BEFORE PRESCRIBING … O. PRIORITISATION") | 10 / 10 | 0 / 10 | Rules A–O are marked as internal guidance, not output headings |
| Extra text after Section 6 (notes, disclaimers, quality checklist, "If you'd like, I can…") | 10 / 10 | 0 / 10 | Output must end after Section 6; checks are internal |
| Raw measurements copied (e.g. "6/30", "1.20 log units") | 10 / 10 | 0 / 10 | No raw measurements anywhere, including Patient Context |
| Low-vision referral added by default | 10 / 10 | 1 / 10 | Only when vision is not correctable and affects detail tasks |
| Driving repeated across sections | 92 mentions | 31 mentions | Driving stated once, briefly, under Education and Support |
| Confirmed fall rated Moderate | 2 of 4 fall cases | 0 of 4 | Falls-risk guide: confirmed fall = High, near-miss/stumble = Moderate. *Taken from the references; needs OT confirmation.* |
| Same recommendation repeated in several sections (2× reference length) | 1419 words | 696 words | Each recommendation stated once |
| No emergency communication / personal alarm | 0 / 10 | 6 / 10 | Support rule adds ability to summon help (phone, personal alarm) |
| Missing information not flagged (e.g. why not driving, stroke extent) | 0 / 10 | 3 / 10 | New rule: say what the OT should clarify instead of assuming |
| Mobility aid not linked to being identified as vision-impaired | 0 / 10 | 4 / 10 | Added to the O&M considerations |
| Useful mobility routines dropped entirely | 1 / 10 | 6 / 10 | Routines allowed as examples of what O&M training may include |

v0.3 already works well for O&M consideration, lighting/contrast, non-visual strategies and factual accuracy, so those rules are unchanged.
